# Code of Conduct

SDP Studio is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free, and professional
community. Participants must be respectful, assume good intent, focus criticism
on ideas and implementation, and avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexualized,
or deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainers listed in `SECURITY.md` or through
the repository's private contact channel. Include links, dates, participants,
and any relevant context; do not include credentials or confidential data.
Reports are acknowledged promptly, reviewed impartially, and handled with
confidentiality to the extent practical. Retaliation against a good-faith
reporter is not acceptable.

Maintainers may take proportionate action, including a private warning,
moderation of contributions, temporary participation limits, or removal from
project spaces. Appeals may be sent to a different maintainer for review.
